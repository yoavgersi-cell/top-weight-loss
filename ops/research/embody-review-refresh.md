### B. Refund-if-not-approved, in numbers
- Refund = amount paid for the current term − value of services already rendered (consultation, provider review, issued prescription, shipped medication, lab services). Embody does not publish a price for those components ("the price is bundled, it is not allocated to or refundable against any individual component"), so the refund amount cannot be computed in advance. Say "a partial refund of the unused portion, less the consult and review", not "full refund".
- 72-hour cancellation cut-off before the next billing date; a request inside the window means one more non-refundable charge.

## Safety and wording notes (for the medical reviewer)
- Compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are not FDA-approved: use embody's own sentence (fact 21) and the FDA Q&A (fact 22, content current 09/16/2025) wherever a price or dose is mentioned.
- Dose language: embody publishes no mg schedule or concentration (house rule 2). Say "doses are set by the OpenLoop Health prescriber; embody does not publish its dose steps or vial strength (mg/mL)". Do not reproduce "units" from reviews as instructions. Do not turn the "2.4 mg/week" footnote into embody's maintenance dose; it describes the population behind a results claim.
