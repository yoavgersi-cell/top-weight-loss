# 2026-11-16-trustpilot-usage — research
Date: 2026-11-16 · Author: Atlas · Status: ready (for Sloane / operator) · Request: operator question after Trustpilot's widget warning (Nov 6, 2026): is a third-party comparison site allowed to display other companies' Trustpilot ratings, under what conditions, does it need a plan or licence, and what must change.

Reading note. Every legal.trustpilot.com URL 301s to corporate.trustpilot.com/legal/…, and support.trustpilot.com 301s to help.trustpilot.com; both targets are refused by the egress proxy, and WebFetch cannot resolve any trustpilot host (only curl through the proxy reaches business.trustpilot.com and developers.trustpilot.com). So the legal clauses below are **search snippets, unverified**, URL and version named; only the pricing and developer pages were read in full. This file reports what the documents say; it is not legal advice.

## 4. Minimal change set, ranked by risk
1. Empty `TRUSTPILOT_WIDGET_ARTICLE_SLUGS` (F): the only live logo mark, recreated stars and full review republication, on another company's profile; matches three clauses directly.
2. Remove quoted review text everywhere it still renders with the flag off: review-analysis excerpts (C), quoted lines in how-to-choose prose and the `go/[slug]` quotes block (gated), `FeaturedReviews` and carousels (code). Replace with the profile link.
3. Decide A/B with Trustpilot in writing: reply to the Nov 6 notice asking whether dated, attributed, text-only TrustScore and count citations with a link are acceptable without an agreement; if not, drop the number and keep "see its Trustpilot profile" + link. Retitle review-analysis pages without "Trustpilot Analysis" if the answer is no.
4. Delete the shipped brand assets (`TrustpilotWordmark` star-mark path, `tpStarColor` scale, star-box renderers) rather than gating them, so no flag can re-enable them.
5. Keep: profile links with nofollow, `sameAs`, no AggregateRating markup, the "trademark of Trustpilot A/S … not affiliated" line, the how-we-rank disclosure.
Cheapest compliant alternative on the texts read: link-only ("Reviews: see welludr's Trustpilot profile"), no number, stars, logo or quotes; or a Data Solutions / content agreement with Trustpilot (terms unreadable here).

## 5. What a paid plan would and would not unlock (pricing page, read 2026-11-16)
Would: TrustBox widgets for **our own claimed domain** ("TrustBox widgets to showcase trust" 9 on Plus at "$319 per month, per domain, billed annually", 11 on Premium at "$799"); testimonial widgets on Premium/Enterprise; "API access" only as an "Add-on" on Premium/Enterprise, terms not reachable. Would not: per the sept-2026 snippet a comparison site "cannot rely on your plan" and needs "its own agreement"; the Business Terms limit display to "domains … you have claimed" with "widgets … we make available to you in your business account". The free account shows "–" for showcase TrustBoxes. Path for third-party data is the developer site's "Data Solutions: Access Trustpilot's global database of business profiles and consumer reviews" (terms/pricing not reachable).
