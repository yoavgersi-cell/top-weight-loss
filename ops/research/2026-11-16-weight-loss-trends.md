## 1. Top 5 trends
1. **FDA class-wide NAION (vision loss) warning.** Drug Safety Communication dated 11/09/2026 requires all GLP-1 RA application holders to add NAION to Warnings and Precautions. Verbatim: meta-analysis of "126 randomized, controlled clinical trials, including 134,253 patients", "15 probable NAION events: 10 in GLP-1 RA patients and 5 in placebo patients", "6.4 events per 100,000 PY in patients treated with GLP-1 RAs compared to 3.8 events per 100,000 PY"; Sentinel cohort vs DPP-4 inhibitors HR 1.43 (95% CI 1.02–2.01), no significant increase vs SGLT-2. Advice: "contact their health care professional right away" for "sudden, painless vision loss in one eye"; HCPs "Consider discontinuing the GLP-1 RA in patients who develop NAION". Source (verbatim): https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-communications/fda-requires-safety-labeling-changes-glucagon-peptide-1-receptor-agonists-glp-1-ras-due-potential (content current 11/09/2026). Labels not yet updated on DailyMed: Wegovy setid ee06186f-2aa3-4990-a760-757579d8f77b v19 "Revised: 6/2026" and Zepbound setid 487cd7e7-434c-4925-99fa-aa80b1cc776b v40 "Revised: 8/2026" list no NAION heading in section 5 (first 100k chars read). ## Safety and wording notes
- NAION: "potential increased risk", "rare but serious"; never "causes blindness". Quote the 6.4 vs 3.8 per 100,000 patient-years with the FDA's population; do not convert to "X times". Do not tell readers to stop; quote the FDA's "contact their health care professional right away".
- Retatrutide: investigational, not FDA-approved; figures are means at week 80 in adults with obesity and type 2 diabetes (TRIUMPH-2), treatment-regimen estimand; do not mix with Lilly's efficacy-estimand 20.8%.
- Compounded: not FDA-approved on every mention; "essentially copies" is the FDA's wording about Empower's products, not a finding about any provider we list.
