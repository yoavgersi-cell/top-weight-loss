## 6. Go / no-go for "Is Eden (TryEden) Legit or a Scam? Pharmacy, Price, Refunds"
Value bar (seo-briefing 1b, edge only from primary sources we have read).

Edge we can document today:
1. FDA letter read at source in full (June 8, 2026, MARCS-CMS 728279), with no response or close-out posted on FDA's list as of Nov 22. Already on reviews/eden; competitors only summarise it.
2. A dated record of Eden's current homepage wording on pharmacies ("state-licensed pharmacies", "FDA-registered labs", "Compounded in the USA"), and the fact that the phrase quoted in the letter does not appear on that homepage. Factual only; no compliance verdict.
3. Eden's own "Same Price at Every Dose" caveat for promotional periods, and the brand-name listings, both of which correct our current pages.
4. The domain move to eden.health.

Edge we cannot document: pharmacy names, Terms entity, prescriber group, cancel steps, refund terms, tirzepatide price, shipping days. The title promises "Pharmacy, Price, Refunds". Three of the five angles a searcher expects ("what pharmacy does tryeden use", "cancel", refund) would be empty, and Cleo's edge case was "pharmacy-naming question the letter raises".

Decision: NO-GO today on the page as titled. GO is conditional on the operator allowlisting www.eden.health, eden.health, app.eden.health and www.edenpharmacy.com, then a re-read of the Terms, FAQ, tirzepatide and semaglutide pages (about one more Atlas pass). In the meantime the corrections in section 5 are worth making on the four existing pages (brand-name claim, "standard rates with no end date", "strict quality oversight", dates). If the operator wants a page now, a narrower "What the FDA said about Eden and what Eden's site says today" is supportable, but without refund or cancel sections.
